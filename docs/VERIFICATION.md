## 1. Content Integrity Principles
1. **No Synthetic Religious Content:** No Quranic verse, Hadith text, narrator chain, or fiqh ruling is generated or improvised by artificial intelligence. All content is sourced from peer-reviewed classical texts.
2. **Honest Status Badging:**
   * **Verified (`VERIFIED`):** Matn, translation, and references have been cross-checked against standard printed editions (e.g. Sahih al-Bukhari, King Fahd Quran).
   * **Pending Review (`AWAITING_REVIEW`):** Items awaiting secondary scholarly sign-off are marked transparently in the UI.
3. **Madhhab Neutrality & Respect:** Hanafi practice is presented as the primary pathway for Bangladesh, while other valid Sunni madhhabs (Shafi'i, Maliki, Hanbali) are explicitly documented and respected without polemics.
