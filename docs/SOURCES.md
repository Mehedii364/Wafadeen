* **Calculation Method:** University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi / Islamic Foundation Bangladesh standard (Fajr angle: 18.0°, Isha angle: 18.0°).
* **Asr Juristic Methods:**
  * Hanafi: Shadow equals twice the object's length plus original noon shadow.
  * Shafi'i / Standard: Shadow equals object's length plus noon shadow.
* **Qibla Bearing:** Great-Circle spherical trigonometric equation referencing the Holy Kaaba in Mecca (`21.4225° N, 39.8262° E`):
  $$\theta = \text{atan2}(\sin(\Delta \lambda), \cos(\phi_1)\tan(\phi_2) - \sin(\phi_1)\cos(\Delta \lambda))$$
